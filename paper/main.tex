\begin{abstract}
We report the construction and execution of a six-stage, validation-gate
pipeline in which an AI agent performed the computationally checkable layer of
quantum-gravity research end-to-end on a single machine: literature mapping,
consistency filtering, consequence computation, reduction checks, formal
verification, and public release. Three independent computational paradigms
were exercised against published ground truth. (i) A two-dimensional
causal-set toolkit reproduced the Myrheim--Meyer ordering fraction
($0.494$--$0.504$ vs.\ the exact $0.5$), matched interval abundances to the
exact analytic series of Machet and Eichhorn (arXiv:2007.13192) to $\sim$1\%
in exact rational arithmetic, and validated the $\varepsilon$-smeared
Benincasa--Dowker--Glaser action, with flat-vs-de~Sitter separation growing
from $1.23\sigma$ ($\varepsilon{=}1$) to $27.5\sigma$ ($\varepsilon{=}0.1$)
and the flat-diamond mean recovering the continuum value $2$ within error bars
($2.19 \pm 0.28$). (ii) An FRG integrator for the Einstein--Hilbert truncation
reproduced the non-Gaussian fixed point $g^* = 0.707321$,
$\lambda^* = 0.193201$ and critical exponents
$\theta = 1.475302 \pm 3.043206\,i$ to all published digits. (iii) A
three-level verification ladder confirmed the Schwarzschild solution of the
vacuum Einstein equations numerically, then symbolically (all 16 Ricci
components identically zero in \texttt{sympy}), then at the level of a
kernel-checked Lean~4 certificate for the underlying algebraic identities.
The pipeline's controls-first design caught two genuine defects during
execution --- a misremembered discrete curvature operator and a silently
incorrect symbolic matrix-inverse routine --- both before they could
contaminate results. We argue that the primary near-term contribution of
agentic tooling to fundamental physics is disciplined verification
infrastructure of this kind, and we document the failure log as a first-class
result. All code, data, and proofs are public and rerunnable.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Claims in fundamental physics are cheap; checkable claims are expensive. The
recent maturation of agentic AI tooling has made it possible for a single
agent, under human direction, to execute large amounts of computational work
--- but the value of that work depends entirely on whether it is disciplined
by explicit validation gates against independently known ground truth.
Without such gates, agent-generated numerics are unfalsifiable prose with
decimal points.

This paper documents a working counterexample: a pipeline in which every
artifact was required to pass an explicit gate --- an exact analytic series, a
published fixed point, a symbolic identity, or a kernel-checked proof ---
before inclusion, and in which failures were logged rather than repaired
silently. We make no claim of new physics. The claim is methodological: the
pipeline ran, the gates held, and two real bugs were caught by the harness
rather than by luck.

\section{Pipeline design}

The pipeline decomposes ``work on fundamental unification'' into six stages:
(1)~literature position check; (2)~consistency filter; (3)~consequence
computation; (4)~reduction check against known limits; (5)~formalization;
(6)~public falsification. Two design rules do the work:

\paragraph{Controls first.} Every instrument must pass a negative control
(does not hallucinate signal) and a positive control (detects a known signal)
before the target is measured.

\paragraph{Explicit kill criteria.} Any artifact failing to recover a known
limit, requiring unlogged trust, or producing no falsifiable output is
rejected or flagged.

\section{Causal-set benchmarks}

A 2D causal-set toolkit (Poisson sprinkling into Minkowski diamonds and
de~Sitter patches, causal-order construction, interval abundances, and the
Benincasa--Dowker--Glaser (BDG) action family) was validated in layers.

\subsection{Sprinkling and causal order}
The Myrheim--Meyer ordering fraction was measured at $N = 100, 200, 400$ over
20-trial ensembles: $0.494$--$0.504$ against the exact 2D value $1/2$,
validating the sprinkling and relation code.

\subsection{Interval abundances against exact theory}
Measured abundances $N_0, N_1, N_2$ were compared against the exact analytic
ensemble expectations (arXiv:2007.13192, eq.~(49)) evaluated in exact rational
arithmetic (\texttt{Fraction}); agreement at $\sim$1\% with zero free
parameters validates the counting code. A residual $2$--$3\sigma$ tension on
two of nine cells is logged as an open item, most plausibly optimistic
standard errors under the heavy-tailed fluctuations documented for these
observables.

\subsection{The $\varepsilon$-smeared BDG action}
The unsmeared 2D action $S = 2(N - 2N_0 + 4N_1 - 2N_2)$ is known to exhibit
large, non-self-averaging fluctuations; we reproduced that behavior and then
implemented the smeared family
\[
  S_\varepsilon = 2\varepsilon\left[N - 2\varepsilon \sum_j f_2(j,\varepsilon)\, N_j\right],
  \qquad
  f_2(j,\varepsilon) = (1-\varepsilon)^j\left[1 - \frac{2j\varepsilon}{1-\varepsilon}
    + \frac{j(j-1)}{2}\left(\frac{\varepsilon}{1-\varepsilon}\right)^{\!2}\right],
\]
with a self-check that $\varepsilon \to 1$ recovers the unsmeared action
exactly. Flat-diamond versus 2D de~Sitter ($R{=}2$) ensemble means
(60 trials, $N{=}400$) separated at $1.23\sigma$ ($\varepsilon{=}1$),
$2.92\sigma$ ($0.5$), $11.30\sigma$ ($0.25$), and $27.54\sigma$ ($0.1$);
at $\varepsilon{=}0.1$ the flat-diamond mean is $2.19 \pm 0.28$, recovering
the continuum value $2$ (the joint/boundary contribution) within error bars.

\paragraph{Logged failure.} The first implementation used a misremembered
per-element operator $B(x) = 2(1 - N_1(x))$; it drifted with $N$, failed the
exact-series gate, and was replaced by the published abundance form. The kill
criteria worked as designed.

\section{Asymptotic safety: Einstein--Hilbert truncation}

FRG flow equations for the Einstein--Hilbert truncation (Litim cutoff, $d{=}4$,
pure gravity; arXiv:2107.01071 eq.~(4.8) at $N_f{=}0$, cross-checked against
the Reuter--Saueressig form) were integrated with a Newton fixed-point finder
and numerical stability analysis. From four independent seeds (spread
$<10^{-16}$, residual $|\beta| \sim 10^{-16}$): the Gaussian fixed point was
verified at the origin, and the non-Gaussian fixed point reproduced published
values to all printed digits: $g^* = 0.707321$, $\lambda^* = 0.193201$,
$\eta_N = -2$ (exact, since $\beta_g = 0$), with critical exponents
$\theta = 1.475302 \pm 3.043206\,i$ matching the published values exactly.
Both $\mathrm{Re}\,\theta > 0$: the NGFP is UV-attractive in both directions
(two relevant couplings). RK4 trajectories integrated toward the UV spiral
into the NGFP (distance $0.054 \to 0.0008$ in 60 steps), consistent with
$\mathrm{Im}\,\theta \neq 0$.

\section{The Schwarzschild verification ladder}

The vacuum Einstein equations $R_{\mu\nu} = 0$ for the Schwarzschild metric
($r > 2M$) were verified at three escalating levels of rigor, each gated by
controls:

\paragraph{Level 0 (numeric).} Richardson-extrapolated ($O(h^4)$) Ricci
computation. Negative control (Minkowski, spherical coordinates): max
$|R_{\mu\nu}| = 1.3\times 10^{-7}$, pass. Positive control (2-sphere of
radius $a$): recovers $R = 2/a^2$ to $10^{-9}$, pass --- proving the checker
detects curvature. Target: max $|R_{\mu\nu}| = 8.0\times 10^{-6}$ over an
18-point grid on $r \in (2M, 20M]$, worst nearest the horizon as expected.

\paragraph{Level 1 (symbolic).} With \texttt{sympy} 1.14.0, all 16 Ricci
components simplify to identically zero --- exact symbolic algebra, not
numerics. \textbf{Logged failure:} the positive control caught that
\texttt{sp.Matrix.inv\_quick} silently returns zeros for symbolic matrices;
the checker uses \texttt{g.inv()}. Without the 2-sphere control, a trivially
``passing'' all-zero result would have been a false positive of exactly the
kind this pipeline exists to prevent.

\paragraph{Level 2 (kernel-checked).} The algebraic identities underlying
$R_{tt} = 0$ and $R_{rr} = 0$ --- rational-function arithmetic over
$\mathbb{Z}[r, M]$, with Christoffel symbols extracted from the Level-1
symbolic computation --- were proved in Lean~4.34.0
(\texttt{Rtt\_vacuum}, \texttt{Rrr\_vacuum}, by \texttt{native\_decide}; the
only axiom is the \texttt{native\_decide} compiler-trust axiom). Scope is
stated honestly: this certifies the algebraic core, not differential geometry
from first principles; the Mathlib manifold-level formalization is identified
as the remaining gap.

\section{Discussion: what the failure log is for}

The two logged defects --- one physical (a misremembered operator), one
computational (a silently wrong library routine) --- were caught by gates, not
by inspection. This is the methodological claim of the paper made concrete:
agent-generated scientific computation is trustworthy exactly to the extent
that its harness is adversarial. We therefore treat the failure log as a
first-class result and publish it alongside the artifacts.

\section{Limitations}

Nothing here is new physics. Reproduction validates the code, not the
underlying research programs; FRG results are scheme- and
truncation-dependent; the Lean certificate covers algebra downstream of
trusted extraction steps; the $2$--$3\sigma$ abundance tension is unresolved;
and all literature positions are a 2026-09-16 snapshot. Conceptual novelty
remains the human's job --- the pipeline filters and hardens ideas, it does
not have them.
